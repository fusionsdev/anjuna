Common Questions

What is Brand Name?

Brand Name is an online lead generation service. We are not a lender, loan broker, or financial institution. When you submit a loan request through our website, your information may be shared with one or more participating lenders, lending partners, affiliate networks, or marketing partners who may contact you with a loan offer. We do not make credit decisions and we do not guarantee that you will receive a loan offer.

Is this a direct lender?

No. Brand Name is not a direct lender. We are an advertising-supported lead generation service. If you submit a request, your information may be shared with third-party lenders and lending partners who will make their own independent credit decisions. We are not involved in the lending decision, loan terms, or funding process.

Does it cost anything to submit a request?

Submitting a loan request through this website is free. Brand Name is compensated by participating lenders and marketing partners. You are never charged a fee by us for submitting a request. However, if you are matched with a lender, the lender may charge origination fees, late payment fees, or other fees as described in their loan agreement. Review the lender's offer carefully before accepting.

How much can I request?

You may submit a loan request for amounts between $350 and $5,000. However, the actual loan amount you may be offered, if any, depends entirely on the lender's review of your application, your creditworthiness, income, state of residence, and the lender's own lending limits. Submitting a request for a specific amount does not guarantee that amount will be offered.

What happens after I submit my request?

After you submit your loan request, your information may be reviewed by participating lenders or partners. If a lender is interested in working with you, they may contact you directly by phone, email, or text message (if you provided consent). They may present you with a loan offer. You are under no obligation to accept any offer. If you accept, the lender will walk you through their application and funding process. Brand Name is not involved in this process after your information is submitted.
