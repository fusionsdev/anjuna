FAQ

What does it cost to check my options?

Checking your options through this site is free. Any lender you choose to work with sets its own fees and terms.

Will every applicant get an offer?

No. Not all applicants qualify, and what is offered depends on the lender, your state and the information you provide.

How much can I look at?

The amount menu lists the amounts you can choose from. Lenders may offer less or more than the amount you pick.

Is my information shared?

See the privacy policy and the notice on selling or sharing personal information linked in the footer for details.
