Fees a lender may charge

  • Origination fee: a one-time fee, often taken out of the amount before the money is sent.
  • Late payment fee: a late or missed payment can add a fee and can lower your credit score.
  • Prepayment fee: some lenders charge for paying early. Many let you pay early at no extra cost.
  • Returned payment fee: a payment that fails for lack of funds can bring a charge from the lender or your bank.

Credit inquiries, renewals and collections

  • Checking your options here does not affect your credit score. A lender may run a soft inquiry, which also does not affect it. Accepting an offer may lead to a hard inquiry, which can.
  • Each lender sets its own renewal policy. A renewal or refinance can add interest and fees.
  • If you do not repay as agreed, the lender may contact you, report to credit bureaus or use a collection agency, as the law allows.
  • Some lenders may be tribal lenders. They follow tribal law and some federal laws, but generally not state law or state rate caps.
